Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026. In short: your photos never leave your device, and we don't track you.

Your photos stay with you

SnapEx is a website that runs entirely in your browser. When you open a photo, your browser reads it, shows its metadata, and builds any edited or cleaned copy on your own device. The photo is never uploaded to SnapEx or to anyone else.

What we don't collect

  • We don't receive, store or see your photos or their metadata.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ups or forms.

Services the page loads

To work, the page loads a few files from other services. Like any website, each of them receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type, but never your photos:

  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps short-lived server logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs serve the open-source libraries that read and write metadata (exifr, piexifjs) and draw the map (Leaflet).
  • OpenStreetMap serves map tiles, only when a photo contains a GPS location. The tiles requested reveal the general area being shown.

If you use Load from URL, your browser downloads the image directly from the address you entered. That site sees the request; SnapEx does not.

Cookies and storage

SnapEx sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. The services above may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Open source

All of SnapEx's code is public on GitHub. You can also open your browser's developer tools while using the site and confirm that no photo data is sent anywhere.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
